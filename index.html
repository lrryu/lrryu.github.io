Position

Accuracy is not provenance.

A land-use label can be correct and still be carried by the district name rather than the street. The audit separates the two.

01 / Selected research

One programme →

Programme

Which view measures which city?

A measurement is a claim about a place. The programme asks where that claim comes from, which view can carry it, and what it may do in a decision.

Read the programme→
01

Seeing and Assuming

A per-location audit of what a vision-language model sees versus what it assumes from the place name — 5,000 street-view points, three open-weight architectures, one spatial field.

 02

Which View Measures Which City

A programme on measurement validity across views: street level, window level, aerial, and rendered. Which view can stand for which construct, and under what conditions?

 03

A Perceptual Layer for Urban Indices

An applied pilot for the Shenzhen Urban Index: turning audited street-level perception into indicators that carry their provenance.

01 / Measure

Read perception from street-level imagery: greenery, openness, interface, vitality, safety.

02 / Audit

Separate what the model sees from what it assumes about the place.

03 / Apply

Turn audited measures into indicators that can be compared, checked, and contested.

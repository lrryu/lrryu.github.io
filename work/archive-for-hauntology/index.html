Installation design and construction

Location
Kunming, China
Year
2023
Type
Built installation
Role
Design team with Yang Zihan, Liu Jianli, Shi Siyuan, Lu Tong
Award
Dianchi Art Season, Wulong Village; built on a ¥10,000 budget

A built installation for the Wulong Village art festival in Kunming. The village was being demolished; the project collected its construction waste — brick, tile, stone, timber — and reassembled it in the original building sequence and surrounding colours.

Placed parallel to the remaining walls and roofs, the new planes read as a montage of a structure that no longer exists. The work treats ruins as material evidence rather than scenery.

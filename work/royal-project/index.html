Royal Project 2.0

Knowledge conservation and preservation in northern Thailand

Location
Chiang Mai, Thailand
Year
2023
Type
Academic studio
Role
Individual project
Award
Selected, HKU Landscape Architecture Annual 2022–23

A study of how traditional agricultural knowledge is eroded when cash-crop systems replace subsistence practice in the hill villages of northern Thailand. The project diagrams five cultivation steps — land preparation, seed saving, soil management, pest control, harvest — against their modern replacements.

It then proposes a knowledge-merging station that lets villagers document, re-evaluate, and adapt practice on site. The drawings treat knowledge as infrastructure: something that can be indexed, transferred, and lost.

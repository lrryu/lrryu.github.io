Static Dynamic

A rural infrastructural device

Location
Chongqing, China
Year
2021
Type
Academic project
Role
Individual design; strategy collaboration with Yang Siqi

A rural infrastructure device for a mountain village in Chongqing. Rather than treating plants as decoration, the project uses the aerial roots of Ficus virens as a construction material that grows into retaining walls, bridges, and handrails over decades.

A point-and-line selection method sets where the network can be installed, while a long-section scenario shows how the device matures with the village. The proposal is deliberately slow: infrastructure that accumulates rather than arrives.

Thanks to Yang Siqi, a collaborator across several projects.

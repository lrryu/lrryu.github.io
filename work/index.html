Selected work · 2021–2024

Eight ways of reading a place.

These projects were made before and alongside the research. They are not portfolio filler: each is an attempt to make a way of reading a place explicit — through water systems, knowledge systems, plant growth, or construction waste. The full portfolio is available as a PDF at the bottom of this page.

012024MLA thesis

Feng Shui Toolkit

Bridging Feng Shui knowledge and landscape ecology

A thesis that asks whether Feng Shui can be read as a knowledge system rather than a set of beliefs. The project reconstructs the historical accumulation of Feng Shui concepts, identifies the parts that correspond to landscape-ecology principles — patch, edge, corridor, mosaic — and translates them into a drawing-based toolkit.

Gold Award (Design Research), 22nd Asian Design Awards

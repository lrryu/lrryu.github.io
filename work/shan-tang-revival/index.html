Revival of Shan Tang

Urban design and landscape section

Location
Suzhou, China
Year
2022
Type
Undergraduate thesis
Role
Landscape team leader; overall coordination
Award
Year UC4+ cross-speciality graduation design

An urban regeneration proposal for the historic Shan Tang area at the foot of Huqiu, Suzhou. Working with historical maps, local gazetteers, and the existing water network, the project identifies the main channels that have persisted and the tributaries that repeatedly shifted, then uses that reading to structure a plan of one water, three rings, nine nodes, and seven districts.

As landscape team leader I coordinated the landscape, architecture, and planning groups and drew the overall plan and section strategy.

Treehouse

Roots grown since the 1970s, uncovered in 2025

Location
Yung Shue Au, Sha Tau Kok, Hong Kong
Year
2025
Type
Village revival · Excavation
Role
Excavation and documentation

Yung Shue Au is a Wen clan village at Sha Tau Kok, Hong Kong. Since the 1970s, the aerial roots of a banyan have grown across a stone wall and formed the opening now called the treehouse. In December 2025, as part of the village revival plan, the site was excavated: old objects were recovered, and the root forms buried under decades of soil were cleared and exposed.

The photographs record the same opening before and after the excavation; a photogrammetric scan records the cleared structure from above. The work is documentation first — a way to see how the roots, the stone wall, the steps, and the path have grown into one another.

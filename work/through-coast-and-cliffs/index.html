Through Coast and Cliffs

Transformational approaches along the coastal trail

Location
Hong Kong, China
Year
2022
Type
Design studio
Role
Collaborative with Zhu Junyan, Qi Yuan, Anna Leung
Award
Selected, HKU Landscape Architecture Annual 2022–23

A landscape study of Hong Kong’s rocky coastal trail, developed with three collaborators. The project reads the cliff as a record of carving: natural erosion, quarrying, and the primitive marks left on rock faces.

It then proposes a trail strategy that cuts and fills with the slope rather than against it, uses excavated rock for breakwaters, and lets walkers continue the carving over time. The drawing set moves between territorial sections and constructed details.
